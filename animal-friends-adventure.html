Privacy Policy for Animal Friends Adventure

Last updated: 26 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how Animal Friends Adventure ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. The app is published by Splec Developers. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children.

1. Information We Collect

The app itself does not ask for, collect, or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Game progress and settings (for example, your current level or sound preferences) are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us.

2. Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use limited technical information such as a device advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location, and app interaction data.

Because this app is directed to children, we have enabled Google's child-directed treatment and request that only family-friendly, non-personalized ads are served. You can learn more about how Google uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Children's Privacy

This app is intended for children and complies with the Google Play Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K) where applicable. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured to be appropriate for a young audience.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share any personal information. The only third-party service used is Google AdMob, for the purpose of displaying advertisements as described above.

5. Data Security

Since the app does not collect personal data, there is no personal data stored on our servers. On-device data stays on your device.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
