Privacy Policy for Doodle Dazzle

Last updated: 27 June 2026

This privacy policy explains how Doodle Dazzle ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use the app. The app is published by Splec Developers. We are committed to protecting the privacy of all users, especially children.

1. Information We Collect

The app itself does not ask for, collect, or store any personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, or precise location. The app does not require an account or sign-in.

Your drawings and creations are stored only on your device and are never transmitted to us. When you choose to save a drawing to your gallery or share it, that action is performed locally on your device using Android's standard share/save features.

2. Advertising

The app displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. To serve ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and use limited technical information such as a device advertising identifier, general (non-precise) location, and app interaction data.

Because this app may be used by children, we have enabled Google's child-directed treatment and request that only family-friendly, non-personalized ads are served. You can learn more about how Google uses this information at https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

3. Permissions Used

  • Storage / Media access — used only to save your drawings to the device gallery when you tap "Save". We do not upload or transmit your drawings.
  • Internet — used only to load advertisements.

4. Children's Privacy

This app is designed to be enjoyable for all ages, including children. We comply with the Google Play Families Policy, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA), and the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR-K) where applicable. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are configured to be appropriate for a young audience.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell or share any personal information. The only third-party service used is Google AdMob, for the purpose of displaying advertisements as described above.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
